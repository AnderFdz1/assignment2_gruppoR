THE PROJECT

Understanding Turin's urban heat

Cool Streets brings local knowledge and research together to understand how heat affects everyday life across the city.

Why this matters

Urban areas can be several degrees warmer than their surroundings. Dense construction, asphalt, limited shade and traffic trap heat, especially in neighbourhoods with fewer trees and public green spaces.

Residents know which streets feel hottest, but those experiences are rarely combined with detailed measurements. This project will connect that local insight with a systematic research approach.

What we will do

  • Invite citizens to identify hot spots and take part in measurement activities.
  • Collect temperature readings across different Turin neighbourhoods.
  • Analyse the data with residents to identify practical cooling opportunities.
01

Better evidence

Open, neighbourhood-level data will help make heat patterns visible and understandable.

02

Healthier streets

Findings can inform choices about shade, planting and public spaces that make streets more comfortable.

03

Shared action

Residents and researchers will build knowledge together and turn it into local conversations.
