UNIVERSITY OF TURIN

Funds TOgether

Crowdfunding for research, innovation and community participation.

Funds TOgether returns for its fourth edition, confirming its role in supporting research and innovation through crowdfunding and creating a bridge between scientific projects and the community.

The new edition opens its doors to research teams, offering training and personalised support to transform projects into effective crowdfunding campaigns, engage civil society and activate the contribution of the university community.

Crowdfunding gives researchers the opportunity to finance their ideas, open their laboratories to the community, communicate their work and build relationships with supporters and partners. It also helps teams develop skills in project management, fundraising and communication.

Funds TOgether was created to bring research closer to society and diversify its sources of funding. The initiative helps research teams turn ideas into concrete projects together with citizens, partners and local communities.

The University of Turin believes in open, participatory and impactful research. Through Funds TOgether, it provides tools, skills and resources to help research teams create effective fundraising campaigns.

2026 PROGRAMME

An opportunity for research teams

Funds TOgether supports research and innovation teams through project selection, campaign development and university co-funding.

Projects selected

HOW THE PROGRAMME WORKS

Four phases, one shared goal

From the first training session to the final co-funding, Funds TOgether supports research teams throughout the process.

01

Training

Research teams take part in practical and interactive training sessions on Ginger crowdfunding.

02

Application and selection

After the training, teams can submit their projects. The University of Turin selects the five best proposals.

03

Support

Ginger campaign managers help selected teams design, implement and promote their crowdfunding campaigns.

04

Co-funding

When a campaign reaches 100% of its goal, the University doubles the funds raised, up to €10,000 per project.
